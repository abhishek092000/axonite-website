The best use of a model in Ledgerline is the one our customers never notice is there.

Operations software should use automation to remove repetitive effort without hiding important decisions. That means clear confidence thresholds, review paths for uncertain results, and an audit trail for actions that change business records.

We start with a measurable bottleneck, test whether automation makes it better, and keep a human in control wherever the cost of a wrong answer is high.
