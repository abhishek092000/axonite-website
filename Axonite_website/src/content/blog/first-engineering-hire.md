date: 2026-05-01

excerpt: "Less about the stack they know, more about how they behave when the stack breaks at 11 pm."
---

Less about the stack they know, more about how they behave when the stack breaks at 11 pm.

Early product teams need engineers who can make sound decisions with incomplete information, communicate clearly, and leave systems easier to understand than they found them.

We value curiosity, care for customers, and a willingness to own the outcome—not just the pull request. A strong first hire helps the whole team improve its judgment.
