Field teams often share one device between two shifts. That single fact changed our entire login flow.

When a phone is a shared tool, the interface must make the active person and their permissions obvious. Switching users should be quick, but not so quick that private information or unfinished work leaks between shifts.

We designed the flow around a clear handover: save the current work, sign out cleanly, and let the next teammate begin without searching through someone else's session. Small operational details like this matter more than another clever animation.
